# M10 Evaluation Templates and One-Click Launch

English | [中文](m10-eval-templates.md)

Turn a fixed evaluation combination (dataset version, agent runtime and assets, model credential, resource pool, and execution policy) into a reusable template, launch it with one click from the evaluations page, and expose three stable APIs for triggering a launch, querying status, and fetching results; templates do not change the execution chain and only turn the existing preparation and creation flow into a repeatable entry point.
